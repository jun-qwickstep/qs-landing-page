7BLead Definition

A "Lead" is a unique individual who matches Client's agreed Ideal Customer Profile and gives a clear, positive response indicating interest in Client's product or service as a direct result of campaign outreach. To qualify, a Lead must satisfy all three:

  1. ICP match. Matches the agreed criteria including job title, company size, industry, and geography stated in the applicable Proposal.
  2. Demonstrated intent. Requests pricing, asks for more information or a link, asks relevant qualification questions, or expresses interest in discussing services.
  3. Real and unique. A real individual, not a generic inbox or role-based email; not duplicated or previously counted.

The following do not qualify and are not counted toward any guarantee or invoice: contacts outside the agreed ICP; students, freelancers, or vendors (unless expressly included in the ICP); spam or bot responses; automated replies or out-of-office messages; duplicate contacts from the same individual or company within ninety (90) days; responses unrelated to the offer; and negative or non-committal responses. Disputes regarding lead validity must be submitted in writing within five (5) business days of delivery. Failure to dispute within this window constitutes acceptance.

7CAppointment Definition and Show-Up Rule

An "Appointment" is a confirmed calendar event with an ICP-matched prospect, scheduled on a calendar system to which Provider has access, where the prospect has explicitly agreed to a meeting time. To qualify, an Appointment must satisfy both:

  1. Scheduled on Client's calendar. The meeting is confirmed and visible on the calendar system Provider has access to, serving as the shared source of truth.
  2. ICP match. Matches the criteria stated in the applicable Proposal. If a prospect outside the agreed ICP slips through, that Appointment does not count toward any guarantee or invoice.

Show-Up. Once an Appointment is confirmed on Client's calendar, Provider's deliverable is complete. Whether the prospect attends is determined by pre-meeting nurture activities (reminders, confirmations, follow-ups), which are Client's responsibility. Confirmed Appointments count toward Client's billable total regardless of attendance.

Reschedules. If the same prospect reschedules one or more times, it still counts as a single Appointment for purposes of guarantee and invoicing.

Cancellations. If a prospect cancels with at least forty-eight (48) hours' notice prior to the scheduled time, the Appointment does not count toward Client's billable total. If the cancellation occurs within forty-eight (48) hours of the scheduled time, the Appointment counts as if attended.

Disputes regarding Appointment validity must be submitted in writing within five (5) business days of invoice issuance. Failure to dispute within this window constitutes acceptance.
