The numbers behind it

Mailbox pricing is InboxKit's published rate card. Domain pricing is what a .com and a .co actually cost at a normal registrar. Change any of it above if your setup differs.

MAILBOXES

You buy a plan with a bucket of slots, then pay per mailbox only for the overflow. Monthly billing runs $39 for 10 slots, $99 for 30, or $299 for 100, with extras at $3.50, $3.25 and $2.99. Annual billing drops the base to $31, $81 and $250 and the extras to $3.10, $2.70 and $2.50. The calculator picks whichever tier lands cheapest at your mailbox count.

PROVIDERS

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 mailboxes cost the same. There's no Microsoft premium. Azure is a different model entirely, priced per tenant with far more mailboxes on a single domain, and this calculator doesn't cover it.

WARMUP

Warmup is a separate $3 per mailbox per month add-on, not part of the plan. If your sending platform already handles warmup, you're paying twice, which is why the toggle starts off.

VOLUME

25 sends per mailbox per day is the ceiling we hold to, across 22 weekdays. It's conservative on purpose. The cost of replacing a burnt domain is much higher than the cost of one more mailbox.

ROUNDING

Mailboxes round up to a whole number of domains, so you never end up with a half-populated domain. That's why the capacity figure sits slightly above your target.
