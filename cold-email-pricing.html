The fine print

Terms that apply
to every offer.

Eight items covering how a lead and an appointment are defined, how the refund guarantee works, what each party is responsible for, and how the engagement starts, switches, and ends.

A unique individual who engages with you through the channels set out in your proposal (or by comment, direct message, or email reply if the proposal doesn't specify), whether they reach out first or respond to outreach we generate, expressing explicit interest in your paid product or service.

Qualifying CTAs: comments or messages responding to a buying-intent call to action count. Things like "Want to implement this system in your business?" or "Want to see how [your product or service] can work for you?" Any call to action that explicitly offers your paid product or service rather than a free resource.

Does not count toward your billable total: generic praise, reactions, or emoji-only responses, comments or messages on content offering a free lead magnet, requests for free resources, spam or bot responses, automated replies or out-of-office, and duplicate contacts from the same individual or company within 90 days.

An appointment counts when it's confirmed and visible on the calendar system we have access to, with the prospect having explicitly agreed to a meeting time. Shared source of truth, no disputes about what was booked when.

Once an appointment lands on your calendar, our part of the work is done. We've sourced the lead, qualified them, handled the back-and-forth, and got the meeting booked. What drives show-up is the pre-call nurture (reminders, confirmations, follow-ups), which sits on your side. We'll help you set it up properly at onboarding.

How edge cases count:

  • Show-up: Bookings count as billable appointments whether or not the lead attends. We're accountable up to the booking; you're accountable for the conversion from there.
  • Reschedules: If the same prospect reschedules once, twice, or four times, it still counts as one appointment. Same person, same conversation, billed once.
  • Cancellation 48h+ before: Doesn't count toward your billable total. That gives us time to rebook or replace the slot.
  • Cancellation within 48h: Counts. At that point the slot is effectively gone.

The two retainer offers come with a hard performance guarantee. If we miss the monthly number, you don't pay for that month.

  • Leads Guaranteed ($1,888/mo). Billed at the start of the month. We commit to 25 qualified leads landing in that month. If we deliver fewer than 25, the month is refunded in full.
  • Appointments Guaranteed ($3,088/mo). Billed at the start of the month. We commit to 10 booked appointments landing on your calendar in that month. If we deliver fewer than 10, the month is refunded in full.

Overdelivery within the month is included at no extra cost. You only get billed once per month, regardless of how many results land beyond the minimum.

It takes 2 to 3 weeks to warm up your sending infrastructure before campaigns can run at full volume. To keep the guarantee fair, the first month doesn't start when you sign. It starts the day campaigns go fully live.
