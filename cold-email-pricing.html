The fine print

Terms that apply
to every offer.

We try to keep the surprises out of cold email. These six items cover how we define a result, what each party is responsible for, and how pause and cancellation work.

A high-intent lead is a prospect who replies to a campaign with explicit interest in your services.

Counts as a lead:

  • "Yes, please send more info"
  • "Send me a calendar link"
  • "What's your pricing?"
  • "Do you work with companies like mine?"

Does not count: bot responses, out-of-office replies, unsubscribes, negative replies, contacts outside the agreed ICP. Each lead must be a real, unique individual matching the ICP.

A booked appointment is a confirmed calendar event with an ICP-matched prospect who has explicitly agreed to a meeting time.

The prospect must be a real and unique individual (not previously booked) and must confirm via calendar acceptance or written confirmation.

Our deliverable ends at the confirmed booking. Show-up rate is not within our control. The pre-meeting nurture sequence (reminders, confirmations, rapport-building) is your responsibility.

How edge cases count:

  • Reschedules: count as one appointment, not two
  • No-shows: still count
  • Cancellations >24h before without reschedule: do not count
  • Cancellations within 24h: count

Active engagements can be paused at $500/month baseline (may be higher based on infrastructure footprint).

Paying the pause fee keeps your domains warm, mailboxes healthy, and the system ready to resume without re-warming. Re-warming a cold system takes ~3 weeks, so a short pause is almost always cheaper than a full restart.

All offers are month-to-month. Either party can end the engagement at the close of any billing cycle. Setup fees and monthly payments are non-refundable.

For the monthly investment offers, if you cancel mid-cycle with the guarantee unmet, the rollover obligation expires on cancellation.

All infrastructure (domains, mailboxes, scrapers, automations, campaign assets) remains the property of QwickStep AI.

On offboarding, we hand over the full lead list scraped during the engagement as a goodwill gesture. The infrastructure itself does not transfer.

Every engagement starts with a 3-week warmup before campaigns go live. The warmup covers domain warming, mailbox setup, list building, copy drafting, and campaign configuration. It's not counted against any guarantee.
